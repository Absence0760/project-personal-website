I build and maintain custom software. Most engagements fall into one of two
streams — commercial work for small businesses, and contract work for
government agencies. Everything is browser-based: no downloadable installers,
no physical goods.

## Custom web & software development

For small businesses, startups, and operators who need something built right
and kept running:

- **Web applications & internal tools** — dashboards, admin panels, and
  workflow tools tailored to how your business actually works.
- **Multi-tenant SaaS** — subscription products with per-customer accounts,
  billing via Stripe, and the supporting infrastructure.
- **E-commerce storefronts** — product catalogues, checkout, and Stripe
  payment integration.
- **Maintenance & modernization** — taking over an existing site or app,
  fixing what's broken, and keeping it secure and up to date.

## Government & federal IT services

I'm establishing the registrations needed to contract with federal agencies.
Capability areas:

- **Custom application development** (NAICS 541511 — Custom Computer
  Programming Services).
- **Computer systems design & integration** (NAICS 541512).
- **Software development & publishing** (NAICS 513210).
- **Test automation & quality engineering** — automated test suites
  (Cypress, Playwright), CI/CD pipelines, and QA process for software
  delivery.

For the full federal profile — core competencies, differentiators, and
corporate data — see the [capability statement](/capabilities/).

## How I work

- **Principal-led.** You work directly with the engineer doing the build —
  not an account manager and an offshore handoff.
- **Security-conscious.** Static-first delivery where it fits, least-privilege
  cloud setups, and no unnecessary third-party dependencies.
- **Transparent.** Clear scope, clear pricing, and ongoing visibility into
  progress.

## Engagement & billing

- **Project fee** — fixed scope, fixed price, for a defined build.
- **Retainer** — ongoing development, maintenance, and support billed monthly.
- **Subscription** — for products I host and operate, billed via Stripe
  Subscriptions.

Billing terms, cancellation, and refunds are covered in the
[Terms of Service](/terms/) and [Refund and Cancellation Policy](/refunds/).
